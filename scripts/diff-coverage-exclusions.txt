# (never a part of a line, so a later untested line of the same shape is not excused); a text under 20
# characters is refused. A short line (`)?;`, `}`) is pinned to its statement by writing the lines above it
# first, joined with `\n`. A backslash escapes the next character, since `|` separates the fields: write `\|`
# for a bar and `\\` for a backslash of the code. The file may hold at most N matches of the text
